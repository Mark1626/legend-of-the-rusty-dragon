          How to play

          Life in the Realm is simple: accept quests, survive them, grow strong, and one day face something big enough to ascend. Everything that happens to you and to everyone else is told in the feed.

          1. Pick a quest from the Bulletin Board that suits your level; the colour of its CR is your guide.
          2. The fight resolves at once, you alone against the whole warband. Victory pays experience and coin; defeat costs nothing but pride and a longer rest.
          3. Rest. Every fight earns one, and you cannot quest again until it ends.
          4. Spend your coin at Absalom's on a better weapon and armour, and keep swinging the same weapon until you master it.
          5. Watch the feed: the Realm stirs on its own, and fortune (or a cutpurse) may find you.
          6. When you can survive a quest whose CR clears the ascension bar shown in the header, you win the game.

          Glossary

          The Realm
          The world of the game and everyone in it. It moves to a heartbeat of a few minutes: quests are posted, events strike, the store restocks, whether or not you are watching.
          The feed
          The Realm's chronicle. Everything public lands here: postings (BOARD), happenings (EVENT), restocks (STORE), and the fights themselves (QUEST). Private news comes to you alone.
          Bulletin Board
          Where quests are posted and claimed. Each carries a four-character number; accepting one starts the fight immediately and takes the posting off the board.
          Quest
          A warband of monsters to face alone. Win and you earn experience and the same amount of coin; lose and you walk away with nothing but a longer rest.
          CR (challenge rating)
          How dangerous a monster is; a quest's CR is the whole warband's added together. Green means at or below your level, amber up to double it, red deadlier still.
          Strategy
          Whom you strike first when facing many: random takes targets at whim, harsh cuts down the weakest first, rage the least dangerous, wise the most dangerous.
          Resting
          Every fight earns one, win or lose, and you cannot quest while it lasts. Higher levels rest longer; defeat adds time, and a hard-won victory shaves some off.
          Level & XP
          Experience follows the D&D fifth-edition table, up to level 20. Each level adds hit points, strength and dexterity.
          Coin
          Won alongside experience, one for one, and occasionally found or stolen from you by events. Spent at the store.
          Absalom's Store
          Weapons and armour in limited stock, replenished over time. Buying replaces what you carry, and nothing is refunded.
          Proficiency
          Fight enough battles with one weapon and you master it: a lasting bonus to hit while you wield it. Costly, heavy weapons take longer to learn, and a new weapon starts the count over.
          AC (armour class)
          How hard you are to hit. Armour sets it, and lighter pieces add some of your dexterity on top.
          Events
          The Realm acting on its own: purses found, potions drunk (blessed or cursed), pockets picked, dragons sighted, whole parties pressed into epic quests.
          Ascension
          Complete a single quest whose CR clears the bar in the header and you win: your adventurer leaves the Realm in glory. Your name stays yours: accept another quest to begin again, fresh at level one.
          The purge
          Go about a week without questing and your adventurer quietly leaves the Realm. Your name and token remain yours; accept a quest to begin anew.
          Token
          Your key to the Realm, shown once when you join and known to nobody else. It cannot be reissued and signing out forgets it, so copy it when it is offered and keep it safe.
